# Quality Risk Analysis

| ID | Risk | Level | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| QR-01 | Learning progress loss | High | Forces rework and damages trust. |
| QR-02 | Incorrect score | High | Misrepresents learning outcomes. |
| QR-03 | Duplicate submission | High | Corrupts score/progress data integrity. |
| QR-04 | Broken resume flow | High | Prevents users from continuing after interruption. |
| QR-05 | Poor error feedback | Medium | Users cannot determine the next action after a failure. |

Priority is based on user impact and data integrity, not on how visually obvious a defect is.
